# EcoRiskAI MVP Design Guide

## Visual Direction
EcoRiskAI should look like a simple environmental intelligence dashboard.

Avoid:
- Overly futuristic AI visuals
- Neon colors
- Complex animations
- Random stock images
- Cluttered charts

Use:
- Clean cards
- Good spacing
- Professional map layout
- Nature-inspired colors

## Colors
Primary:
- Forest Green: #2E7D32
- Emerald: #43A047

Neutral:
- Slate: #334155
- Light Gray: #F8FAFC
- Border Gray: #E2E8F0
- White: #FFFFFF

Risk colors:
- Low: #22C55E
- Medium: #F59E0B
- High: #EF4444

## Typography
Use a clean sans-serif font.

Recommended:
- Inter
- system-ui
- Arial fallback

## Layout
Desktop-first.

Dashboard:
- Top navbar height: 56px
- Left sidebar width: 280px
- Map fills remaining space
- Right drawer width: 360px

## Components
Cards:
- Rounded corners
- Light border
- Subtle shadow
- White background

Buttons:
- Primary: forest green background
- Secondary: white with gray border

Charts:
- Keep simple
- Avoid too many colors
- Use clear labels

## Tone
The copy should sound:
- Clear
- Honest
- Research-oriented
- Useful to conservation teams

Avoid claims like:
“This predicts deforestation perfectly.”

Use:
“This prototype estimates relative ecosystem risk using available environmental data.”
